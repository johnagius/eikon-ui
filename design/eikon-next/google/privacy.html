Google Calendar connection

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. Eikon does not need it to work. If you choose Connect Google Calendar on your profile page, Eikon asks Google for permission to create one calendar in your Google account and manage the events in it. Google calls this permission calendar.app.created. On Google's consent screen it reads: "Make secondary Google calendars, and see, create, change, and delete events on them."

What Eikon does with this access

  • Creates one calendar called "Eikon rota" in your Google account.
  • Adds your own rota shifts and approved leave to that calendar as events.
  • Updates or removes those events when the rota changes or leave is approved or cancelled, usually within seconds.

What Eikon cannot and does not do

  • Eikon cannot see, read, change or delete your other calendars or events. The permission covers only the calendar Eikon created.
  • Eikon does not read your Google email, contacts, files or any other Google data.
  • Eikon does not put other staff members' shifts in your calendar, and does not put yours in theirs.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

  • Disconnect at any time from your Eikon profile. Eikon then revokes its access with Google and deletes the stored token, calendar ID, connected email address and event IDs immediately. You can choose to have the "Eikon rota" calendar deleted from your Google account as well. If you don't, it stays, and Eikon no longer updates it.
  • You can also remove Eikon's access from Google's side at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Eikon then stops updating the calendar and deletes the stored token the next time it tries to use it.
  • When a staff member leaves and their Eikon account is closed, the Google connection is removed in the same way.
  • Database backups kept by our hosting provider may hold a copy for up to N DAYS before they are overwritten.

Sharing

Google data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising. It is processed only on Eikon's servers, hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., to provide the calendar feature. It may be disclosed only if the law requires it.

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Eikon's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, data received from Google:

  • is used only to provide the user-facing "Eikon rota" calendar feature described above;
  • is not transferred to others, except as needed to provide that feature, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale of Eikon with the user's data protected in the same way;
  • is not used or transferred for advertising, including retargeting and personalised or interest-based ads;
  • is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending;
  • is not used to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models;
  • is not read by people, unless you give your permission for specific data (for example to fix a problem you report), it is needed for security (for example investigating abuse), it is needed to comply with the law, or it has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

Security

Connections to Eikon and to Google use HTTPS. Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest, and access to Eikon's production systems is limited to WHO, e.g. the operator.
