# Eikon — A day at the pharmacy: overview film script

The beat sheet: each sentence with its stressed words (CAPS), its pauses (marks and milliseconds) and the on-screen action cued to a word. Served locally from the current live code (S/live, main b2edf54: UI .183, worker batch-20) with the curated busy seed plus this film's story data (`scripts/film-extras-overview.mjs`); the live site was never contacted.

Length 85.9 s · 1920×1080, 30 fps (captured at 2560×1440, device scale 4/3) · voice C (Chatterbox, prompted with the clean synthetic #4 clip, as the approved Registers pilot) · narration mastered to -16 LUFS.

**The story.** One pharmacy's day, calmly: Today says only what needs someone; the fridge is logged from Today; every register is kept and the whole inspection pack prints in one tap; a controlled drug given in parts is remembered and the rest filled in; at closing time one button opens the end of day, counted, the difference in words, locked; a second vaccine dose due today, filled in and saved; the rota: a usual week set once and a whole year put on the rota, and everyone's shifts on their own phone; a customer's whole story and loyalty card in one place; then the distributor's and head office's screens; and the promise, with the sign-in page's one-click demo.

**Narrator's marks** (as the pilot): CAPS = stressed word · | = short pause · ‖ = sentence pause · ‖‖ = held beat · … = held (level) ending. "Eikon" is said EYE-kon. Authoritative, warm, unhurried (John asked for a narrator "really strong, like Robert Powell": the voice is still voice C, nobody's voice is imitated); short sentences written for the ear, deliberate pauses, the stressed word carried in each phrase (measured: 112 words a minute overall, pauses included; sentences at about 120–150).

Each sentence was recorded as several takes; the take used, its scores and the pause marks it was fitted to are in `timing.json` (`chosen`). Spoken times are the faster-whisper word timings on the mastered narration; each action is cued to the word in **bold**.
