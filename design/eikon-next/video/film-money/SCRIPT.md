# Eikon — Money & end of day: section film script

Length 67.3 s · 1920×1080, 30 fps (captured at 2560×1440, device scale 4/3) · voice C (Chatterbox, prompted with the clean synthetic #4 clip, as the approved Registers pilot) · narration mastered to -16 LUFS.

**The story.** Closing time. Today's money says what is left to do; the courier is paid from the till and written down once; the end of day goes in steps (readings, the paid outs pulled in, the count), says the difference in words with what to check, and is locked; then, on their own, the emergency till that keeps selling when the main till is down (it is not part of the end of day), and a credit note used at the counter.

**Narrator's marks** (as the pilot): CAPS = stressed word · | = short pause · ‖ = sentence pause · ‖‖ = held beat · … = held (level) ending. "Eikon" is said EYE-kon. Plain, warm, confident; short sentences; about 130 words a minute overall (measured: 119 words a minute, pauses included).

Each sentence was recorded as several takes; the take used, its scores and the pause marks it was fitted to are in `timing.json` (`chosen`). Spoken times are the faster-whisper word timings on the mastered narration; each action is cued to the word in **bold**.
